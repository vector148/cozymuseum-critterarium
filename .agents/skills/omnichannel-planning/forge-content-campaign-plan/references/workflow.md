# Campaign Forge Workflow

This reference describes the end-to-end department handoff sequence. Omnichannel Planning owns
scope, truth/evidence intake, strategy, and the contracts sent to specialists. It does not absorb
Specialized Execution or CRO: execution artifacts return through typed handoffs, and CRO alone
evaluates performance evidence and issues the learning decision.

## 1. Intake And Omnichannel Planning Gate

Capture the request, owner, deadline, budget, founder capacity, desired customer change, selected product/capability when applicable, and approval path. Explicitly mark brand-only or education-only work so the plan never invents a product claim. Write one scope statement:

```text
For [persona] in [market], during [period], use [canonical asset + approved channels]
to achieve [one journey objective] within [cash/time ceiling], while excluding [out-of-scope work].
```

Reject “post more”, “go viral”, “be everywhere”, or a deliverable count as the primary objective.

Output: approved scope, exclusions, assumptions, risk owners, and revalidation date.

## 2. Truth Pack

Build three separate records:

1. **Brand truth:** canonical Brand DNA, four knowledge pillars, No-Guru boundary, regional voice.
2. **Product truth:** verified capability, version, limitation, availability, proof artefact, Product Vault owner.
3. **Founder truth:** immutable `insight_id`, thesis, emotional tension, intended reader change, approval state. Strategic campaign work cannot substitute generic AI or agency language for this layer. Mandatory release, legal, service, or support communication may instead cite an approved operational source and must be classified `operational_communication`.

Do not merge judgement, fact, and hypothesis into one paragraph.

Gate: no campaign promise may exceed these three records.

## 3. Evidence Pack

Use dated, attributable sources. For each material claim record:

- source and date;
- geography and audience;
- observation;
- interpretation;
- contradiction or counter-hypothesis;
- limitation;
- confidence;
- expiry or next validation date.

Cover the primary persona, decision moment, competitor/alternative set, search/channel behaviour, objections, and product relevance. Use two independent sources before promoting a pattern to recommendation when feasible.

Gate: an undated or untraceable claim remains a lead, not evidence.

## 4. Strategic Synthesis

### 4.1 Target Audience

Select exactly one primary persona. Describe job, decision moment, friction, evidence need, objection, channel behaviour, prohibited inference, and next test. Secondary personas may be recorded but may not dilute the key message.

### 4.2 Competitors And Alternatives

Compare by customer job and trust, not by superficial feature count. Include:

- named professional tools;
- generic MQL5 alternatives;
- manual workflows;
- free education/content;
- doing nothing.

For each, record audience, promise, proof, acquisition path, retention mechanism, strength, weakness, and Atumerce response. Do not copy a competitor's claim, creative motif, or visual system.

### 4.3 SWOT And Constraints

Limit each quadrant to the few items that change a decision. Every row needs evidence, implication, owner, and action. Add capacity, privacy, phase, product-release, and founder-review constraints.

Gate: reject generic SWOT items such as “strong brand” or “competitive market” without evidence and implication.

## 5. Master Plan

Create campaign rows in this exact dependency order:

`timeline -> objective -> decision event -> persona -> insight -> context clocks -> strategic approach -> big idea/key message -> content direction -> tactic -> activity -> evidence -> metric -> review decision`

Use one primary objective and one bounded key message per campaign. Choose `Diagnose`, `Educate`, `Prove`, `Adopt`, `Retain`, or `Learn` as the campaign stage.

Resolve three clocks before choosing tactics: the market calendar, audience attention rhythm, and Atumerce product/release calendar. A clock belongs in the plan only when it changes timing, evidence, risk, or the customer's decision; do not imitate consumer-brand seasonality.

Gate: the master plan is the production control surface. Any change to persona, objective, canonical asset, channel count, claim boundary, deadline, or resource ceiling is a scope change.

## 6. Content Guideline

Assign two labels to every asset:

1. one primary knowledge pillar: Price Action, Allocation, Macro, or Psychology;
2. one campaign role: Authority/Market Truth, Product Logic/Workflow, Decision Education, Trust/Operating Evidence, or Commercial Activation.

Record tone, proof, allowed wording, forbidden leap, visual logic, channel behaviour, language, CTA, accessibility, media rights, and review owner.

Gate: commercial activation must not dominate the mix and may not exist without evidence.

## 7. Calendar And Capacity

Schedule the canonical source before derivatives. Every calendar row records publication window, review deadline, owner, reviewer, source package, format, channel, language, visual requirement, founder minutes, production hours, cost, CTA, status, and outcome.

Use three clocks:

- market calendar;
- audience attention rhythm;
- Atumerce product/release calendar.

Leave recovery room. Reduce output when review capacity is unavailable.

Gate: a calendar is valid when it protects evidence and capacity, not when every date is filled.

## 8. Execution Handoff

Create specialist handoffs with input, output, owner, dependency, review state, and acceptance criteria. Phase 2 may produce drafts and packages. Phase 3 owns approved publication connectors and recovery.

Minimum status flow:

`proposed -> researched -> scoped -> approved_for_draft -> drafted -> reviewed -> approved -> scheduled -> published -> measured -> archived`

Use `blocked` at any state and record the blocker owner.

## 9. Measurement Contract And CRO Handoff

Planning defines the business question, eligible population, metric contract, guardrails, and evidence
needed before execution. After observed evidence returns, CRO—not Planning—records baseline,
consent posture, event taxonomy, confounders, cost, founder time, review date, and the
`continue/adapt/pause/stop/archive/escalate` decision. Prefer qualified action and learning over reach.

Never join anonymous analytics to CRM without explicit purpose and consent. Preserve negative results and contradictory evidence.

## 10. Final Release Gate

Run `commercial-content-audit`. Block release on missing lineage, weak evidence, unsupported financial claim, privacy ambiguity, inaccessible media, channel mismatch, unowned review, or a zero in Brand DNA/product truth/evidence/privacy.

artifact thực thi quay lại qua typed handoff, và chỉ CRO đánh giá evidence hiệu suất rồi ban hành

### 1. Intake Và Omnichannel Planning Gate

Ghi request, owner, deadline, budget, founder capacity, thay đổi khách hàng mong muốn, product/capability được chọn khi có và approval path. Đánh dấu rõ work brand-only hoặc education-only để plan không bịa product claim. Viết một scope statement:

```text
Cho [persona] tại [market], trong [period], dùng [canonical asset + channel đã duyệt]
để đạt [một journey objective] trong [cash/time ceiling], đồng thời loại trừ [out-of-scope work].
```

Từ chối “post nhiều hơn”, “viral”, “có mặt mọi nơi” hoặc số deliverable làm primary objective.

Đầu ra: scope đã duyệt, exclusion, assumption, risk owner và revalidation date.

### 2. Truth Pack

Tạo ba record riêng:

1. **Brand truth:** Brand DNA canonical, bốn knowledge pillar, No-Guru boundary và regional voice.
2. **Product truth:** capability, version, limitation, availability, proof artefact và Product Vault owner đã kiểm chứng.
3. **Founder truth:** `insight_id`, thesis, emotional tension, thay đổi người đọc dự kiến và approval state bất biến. Strategic campaign không được dùng generic AI/agency language thay lớp này. Communication bắt buộc về release, legal, service hoặc support có thể dùng operational source đã duyệt và phải phân loại `operational_communication`.

Không trộn judgement, fact và hypothesis trong một paragraph.

Gate: campaign promise không được vượt ba record này.

### 3. Evidence Pack

Dùng source có ngày và attribution. Với mỗi claim quan trọng, ghi:

- source và date;
- geography và audience;
- observation;
- interpretation;
- contradiction hoặc counter-hypothesis;
- limitation;
- confidence;
- expiry hoặc next validation date.

Bao phủ persona chính, decision moment, competitor/alternative set, search/channel behaviour, objection và product relevance. Khi khả thi, dùng hai source độc lập trước khi nâng pattern thành recommendation.

Gate: claim không ngày hoặc không trace được chỉ là lead, chưa phải evidence.

### 4. Tổng hợp chiến lược

#### 4.1 Target Audience

Chọn đúng một persona chính. Mô tả job, decision moment, friction, evidence need, objection, channel behaviour, prohibited inference và next test. Có thể ghi persona phụ nhưng không được làm loãng key message.

#### 4.2 Competitor Và Alternative

So sánh theo customer job và trust, không theo feature count bề mặt. Bao gồm:

- professional tool có tên;
- alternative MQL5 phổ thông;
- manual workflow;
- education/content miễn phí;
- không làm gì.

Với mỗi alternative, ghi audience, promise, proof, acquisition path, retention mechanism, strength, weakness và response Atumerce. Không copy claim, creative motif hoặc visual system của competitor.

#### 4.3 SWOT Và Constraint

Giới hạn mỗi quadrant ở vài item thật sự thay đổi decision. Mỗi row cần evidence, implication, owner và action. Thêm constraint capacity, privacy, phase, product-release và founder-review.

Gate: từ chối item SWOT chung chung như “strong brand” hoặc “competitive market” khi thiếu evidence và implication.

### 5. Master Plan

Tạo campaign row theo đúng dependency:

`timeline -> objective -> decision event -> persona -> insight -> context clocks -> strategic approach -> big idea/key message -> content direction -> tactic -> activity -> evidence -> metric -> review decision`

Dùng một primary objective và một key message có giới hạn cho mỗi campaign. Chọn `Diagnose`, `Educate`, `Prove`, `Adopt`, `Retain` hoặc `Learn` làm campaign stage.

Giải quyết ba clock trước khi chọn tactic: market calendar, audience attention rhythm và Atumerce product/release calendar. Chỉ đưa clock vào plan khi nó thay đổi timing, evidence, risk hoặc customer decision; không bắt chước seasonality của consumer brand.

Gate: master plan là production control surface. Mọi thay đổi persona, objective, canonical asset, channel count, claim boundary, deadline hoặc resource ceiling đều là scope change.

### 6. Content Guideline

Gắn hai label cho mỗi asset:

1. một knowledge pillar chính: Price Action, Allocation, Macro hoặc Psychology;
2. một campaign role: Authority/Market Truth, Product Logic/Workflow, Decision Education, Trust/Operating Evidence hoặc Commercial Activation.

Ghi tone, proof, allowed wording, forbidden leap, visual logic, channel behaviour, language, CTA, accessibility, media right và review owner.

Gate: commercial activation không được thống trị content mix và không được tồn tại khi thiếu evidence.

### 7. Calendar Và Capacity

Schedule canonical source trước derivative. Mỗi calendar row ghi publication window, review deadline, owner, reviewer, source package, format, channel, language, visual requirement, founder minutes, production hours, cost, CTA, status và outcome.

Dùng ba clock:

- market calendar;
- audience attention rhythm;
- Atumerce product/release calendar.

Chừa recovery room. Giảm output khi không đủ review capacity.

Gate: calendar hợp lệ khi bảo vệ evidence và capacity, không phải khi lấp đầy mọi date.

### 8. Handoff thực thi

Tạo specialist handoff có input, output, owner, dependency, review state và acceptance criteria. Phase 2 được tạo draft/package. Phase 3 sở hữu publication connector và recovery đã duyệt.

Status flow tối thiểu:

`proposed -> researched -> scoped -> approved_for_draft -> drafted -> reviewed -> approved -> scheduled -> published -> measured -> archived`

Dùng `blocked` ở bất kỳ state nào và ghi blocker owner.

### 9. Contract Đo Lường Và Handoff Sang CRO

Planning xác định business question, eligible population, contract metric, guardrail và evidence cần
có trước execution. Sau khi evidence quan sát quay lại, CRO—không phải Planning—ghi baseline,
consent posture, event taxonomy, confounder, cost, founder time, review date và decision
`continue/adapt/pause/stop/archive/escalate`. Ưu tiên qualified action và learning hơn reach.

Không nối anonymous analytics vào CRM khi chưa có purpose và consent rõ. Giữ negative result và contradictory evidence.

### 10. Gate phát hành cuối

Chạy `commercial-content-audit`. Block release khi thiếu lineage, evidence yếu, financial claim không được hỗ trợ, privacy mơ hồ, media không accessible, channel mismatch, review không owner hoặc điểm 0 ở Brand DNA/product truth/evidence/privacy.
